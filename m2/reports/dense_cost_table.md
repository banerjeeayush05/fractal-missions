## 5. Reading the table

- The M2.4 gate case is small: one fp64 field is 21.6 MB, so the peak-memory gate of 40 GB
  (decision §7) is met by simple two-level checkpointing for any plausible k, and the
  three-level scheme — with its second recompute and the clash with the adjoint-ratio
  target — is not needed. This is the main consequence of the decision §2 rescoping.
- `jax.checkpoint` on the step function alone still stores all N carries, so it costs as
  much as the naive figure. Two-level means a nested scan (decision §7, OPEN_QUESTIONS A4).
- k, the number of field-sized residuals kept per step, is still unmeasured. Measure it at
  M2.3 and rerun this table before the M2.4 gate numbers are finalised.
- Phase-2 M01 at dx = 2 nm is the largest case in the mission and still modest in 2D. Its
  3D form (1270×2000×2000) would be 40 TB per field and is not planned; phase 2 is 2D.
- On this 8 GB M1, the 2D cases fit comfortably and the 3D gate case fits for the forward
  pass; the gate itself belongs on the H100 (decision §6).
